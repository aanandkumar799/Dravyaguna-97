Sources & Review

A public record of how DravyaGuna 97 handles sources, verification, missing information and updates.

Page reviewed: 22 September 2026

Primary academic basis

  • NCISM BAMS Dravyaguna syllabus/list: the core library is organized as the canonical 97-drug NCISM series.
  • Classical Ayurvedic literature: Samhita and Nighantu references are displayed only when recorded in the individual plant dossier.
  • Authoritative botanical references: botanical identity is cross-checked where taxonomy changes or historical nomenclature creates ambiguity.

Secondary structured data

Amidha Ayurveda Herb Database v2.0 is used only as a secondary structured source to fill blank fields where its data supports the existing record. It is not treated as a replacement for classical or authoritative verification.

License recorded in the project: CC BY 4.0.

Botanical cross-checking

Current botanical taxonomy may differ from names used in older Ayurvedic textbooks. Where this occurs, the educational/textbook name can be retained for continuity while the current botanical name is identified separately.

Example: Agnimantha taxonomy was cross-checked against Kew's Plants of the World Online.

Review & attribution

DravyaGuna 97 does not invent reviewer credentials. A named reviewer is shown only when a reviewer is explicitly recorded in the underlying project data.

Project status: maintained educational database; individual records can have different verification status.

Independent academic reviewer: not yet assigned. A real BAMS/Ayurveda faculty reviewer should be added only after their consent and actual review of the relevant records; no reviewer identity is fabricated.

Verification rules

  1. Missing information is shown as unavailable rather than fabricated.
  2. Textbook material is paraphrased for study use rather than copied as a page-for-page reproduction.
  3. Classical quotations, chapter/verse references and doses should be cross-checked against the cited edition before academic or clinical use.
  4. Taxonomic changes are documented separately from historical textbook nomenclature when relevant.
  5. Image records should identify their source where a verified external source is used.
Important medical-education disclaimer: DravyaGuna 97 is a study and revision resource, not a clinical decision system. Plant identification, therapeutic indications, doses, contraindications and formulations must not be used as a substitute for qualified clinical assessment, prescribing/dispensing supervision, pharmacopoeial standards, or current professional guidance.
