Asthishrunkhala

Cissus quadrangularis L.
NCISM 97 · No. 44

A distinctive succulent climber with quadrangular, jointed green stems; tendrils arise at nodes and small leaves occur on young shoots.

Quick revision
Asthishrunkhala (Cissus quadrangularis L., Vitaceae) is a succulent climber recognized by its four-angled jointed stem; the stem is the principal traditional medicinal part and is associated with asthisandhanaka use.
