Vacha

Acorus calamus L.
NCISM 97 · No. 91

Sub-cylindrical tortuous branched rhizomes marked with triangular leaf scars and rootlets, brownish exterior and whitish spongy interior, strong peculiar aromatic odor and warm pungent bitter taste.

Verified study data

Sanskrit name
वचा
Botanical name
Acorus calamus L.
Family
Acoraceae
English name
Sweet Flag / Calamus
Habit
Perennial semi-aquatic, aromatic herb with a creeping branched rhizome and sword-shaped leaves.
Description
Sub-cylindrical tortuous branched rhizomes marked with triangular leaf scars and rootlets, brownish exterior and whitish spongy interior, strong peculiar aromatic odor and warm pungent bitter taste.
Rasa
katu, tikta
Guna
laghu, tikshna
Virya
ushna
Vipaka
katu
Useful part
Rhizome (Kanda)
Indications
Unmada (Psychosis / Mental confusion), Apasmara (Epilepsy), Swarabheda (Hoarseness of voice), Smritibhramsha (Memory impairment), Agnimandya (Loss of appetite), Vibandha (Constipation / Abdominal distension)
Dose
Churna 60–120 mg (API; after shodhana only); 1–2 g for vamana
Anupana
Madhu (honey) or Ksheera (milk) for Medhya use
Precautions
Ushna-Tikshna drug with emetic action in larger doses: use only in prescribed dose and preferably after shodhana. Avoid long-term or high-dose use; the β-asarone content of tetraploid (Indian) chemotypes is a safety concern. Use cautiously in Pitta disorders.
Contraindications
Pregnancy and lactation, Raktapitta and bleeding states, Pitta-predominant conditions, and young children except in small classical doses under supervision.
Therapeutic actions
Medhya, Sanjnasthapana, Swarya, Vamana, Deepana, Pachana, Kaphaghna, Unmadahara
Quick revision
Vacha (Acorus calamus): rhizome; Katu-Tikta rasa, Laghu-Tikshna guna, Ushna virya, Katu vipaka; Kapha-Vata shamaka; Medhya, Vamaka, Sanjnasthapana; key in Apasmara, Unmada, speech disorders.
Shloka / classical text
वचोग्रगन्धा कटुका तिक्तोष्णा वान्तिवह्निकृत् । विबन्धाध्मानशूलघ्नी शकृन्मूत्रविशोधिनी ॥ अपस्मारकफोन्मादभूतजन्त्वनिलान् हरेत् ॥
Nighantu references
Bhavaprakasha Nighantu, Haritakyadi Varga, Verse 102-103
