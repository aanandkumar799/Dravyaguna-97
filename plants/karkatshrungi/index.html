Quick revision
Karkatashringi is the characteristic gall of Pistacia integerrima. The gall is the useful part; the supplied textbook records Kashaya-Tikta rasa, Ushna virya, Katu vipaka and uses in conditions including Kasa, Shwasa and Hikka.
