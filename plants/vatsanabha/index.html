Vatsanabha

Aconitum ferox Wall. ex Ser.
NCISM 97 · No. 94

Conical tuberous root resembling a calf's navel, dark brown exterior with longitudinal wrinkles, whitish or pinkish internal fracture; highly toxic in unpurified state.

Verified study data

Sanskrit name
वत्सनाभ
Botanical name
Aconitum ferox Wall. ex Ser.
Family
Ranunculaceae
English name
Monkshood / Indian Aconite
Habit
Erect perennial herb, 1–2 m tall, arising from paired tuberous roots.
Description
Conical tuberous root resembling a calf's navel, dark brown exterior with longitudinal wrinkles, whitish or pinkish internal fracture; highly toxic in unpurified state.
Rasa
madhura
Guna
laghu, ruksha, tikshna, vikasi, vyavayi
Virya
ushna
Vipaka
katu
Useful part
Purified Tuberous Root (Shodhita Kanda)
Indications
Jwara (Acute & chronic fever / Intermittent fever), Sandhishoola (Severe joint pain / Arthralgia), Amavata (Rheumatoid arthritis), Kasa (Cough / Bronchial spasm), Shwasa (Dyspnea / Asthma), Vatavyadhi (Neurological disorders / Sciatica)
Dose
15–30 mg (shodhita only; API) — after shodhana only
Anupana
Not available in verified record
Precautions
Highly poisonous Sthavara visha (Kanda visha). Use internally ONLY after classical shodhana (gomutra bhavana for 3 days, or Dola yantra swedana in gomutra or godugdha) and only in the API dose of 15–30 mg of shodhita drug, almost always within a formulation. Exceeding the dose is dangerous. Ashodhita Vatsanabha, even in small amounts, can cause tingling and numbness of mouth and limbs, vomiting, hypotension, arrhythmias and death. To be prescribed only by a qualified physician; never for self-medication.
Contraindications
Ashodhita (unpurified) drug in any form; pregnancy and lactation; children and the elderly or debilitated; heart disease, arrhythmias or hypotension; Pitta-predominant conditions, Raktapitta and Daha; hot season and use with heat exposure; patients with poor Agni or debility; long-term use.
Therapeutic actions
Yogavahi, Vedanasthapana, Jwarahara, Swedana, Deepana, Pachana, Rasayana, Vyavayi
Quick revision
Processed aconite, used in fevers, pain and digestion.
Shloka / classical text
वत्सनाभो अतिविषवत् ज्ञेयः कफविषापहः। बल्यो रसायनो वृष्यो वातजिच्च महागुणः॥
