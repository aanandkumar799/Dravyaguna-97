Shunthi

Zingiber officinale Roscoe
NCISM 97 · No. 85

Dried, buff to light-grey, branched rhizome with characteristic aromatic pungency.

Verified study data

Sanskrit name
शुण्ठी
Botanical name
Zingiber officinale Roscoe
Family
Zingiberaceae
English name
Dry Ginger
Habit
Perennial rhizomatous herb with leafy pseudostems 30–90 cm tall.
Description
Dried, buff to light-grey, branched rhizome with characteristic aromatic pungency.
Rasa
katu
Guna
laghu, snigdha
Virya
ushna
Vipaka
madhura
Useful part
dried rhizome (Shushka Kanda)
Indications
Amavata, Agnimandya, Adhmana, Chhardi, Kasa, Shoola
Dose
Churna 1–2 g
Anupana
Ushna jala (warm water); with Guda in Amavata
Precautions
Ushna-Tikshna drug: use cautiously in Pitta-predominant states, Raktapitta, Amlapitta, Daha and bleeding tendencies, and in summer. Large doses may cause heartburn.
Contraindications
Raktapitta and active bleeding, severe Amlapitta and peptic ulcer, Pittaja Jwara with burning; use cautiously with anticoagulant medicines.
Therapeutic actions
Deepana, Pachana, Anulomana, Amadoshahara, Vatakaphahara, Hridya, Vrishya
Quick revision
Dry ginger is known as the 'universal medicine'. It aids digestion, reduces inflammation, and pacifies Vata and Kapha.
Shloka / classical text
शुण्ठी रुच्यामवातघ्नी पाचनी कटुका लघुः । स्निग्धोष्णा मधुरा पाके कफवातविबन्धनुत् ॥४५॥
Nighantu references
Bhavaprakasha Nighantu, Haritakyadi Varga, verse 45; related therapeutic continuation in verse 46. Independently searchable recension; edition numbering may vary.
Samhita references
Ashtanga Hridaya, Sutrasthana 6/163–164
