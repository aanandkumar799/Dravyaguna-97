Katuki

Picrorhiza kurroa Royle ex Benth.
NCISM 97 · No. 34

Sub-cylindrical grayish-brown rhizomes marked with prominent circular leaf-scars and stem remnants, porous white internal fracture, intensely bitter persistent taste.

Verified study data

Sanskrit name
कटुका
Botanical name
Picrorhiza kurroa Royle ex Benth.
Family
Plantaginaceae (Scrophulariaceae)
English name
Picrorhiza / Gentian
Habit
Small perennial hairy herb with long creeping rhizome; alpine Himalaya 3000–5000 m
Description
Sub-cylindrical grayish-brown rhizomes marked with prominent circular leaf-scars and stem remnants, porous white internal fracture, intensely bitter persistent taste.
Rasa
tikta
Guna
laghu, ruksha
Virya
sheeta
Vipaka
katu
Useful part
Rhizome / Root (Kanda / Mula)
Indications
Kamala (Jaundice / Hepatitis), Yakridroga (Hepatic & splenic disorders), Jwara (Chronic fever / Malarial fever), Kushta (Skin diseases / Dermatitis), Vibandha (Constipation), Raktapitta (Bleeding disorders)
Dose
Churna 1–3 g (API); 3–6 g as bhedana
Anupana
Not available in verified record
Precautions
Bhedana in larger doses; may cause griping and loose stools. Commonly adulterated or substituted with Gentiana kurroo, so authenticate the rhizome.
Contraindications
Pregnancy, Atisara, marked Vata predominance and debility (Laghu-Ruksha, Bhedana drug).
Therapeutic actions
Bhedana, Deepana, Pachana, Jwarahara, Yakriduttejaka, Raktashodhaka, Hridya
Quick revision
Katuki — Picrorhiza kurroa Royle ex Benth.; Plantaginaceae (Scrophulariaceae); part: Rhizome / Root (Kanda / Mula); Rasa tikta; Guna laghu, ruksha; sheeta virya; katu vipaka.
Shloka / classical text
कट्वी तु कटुका तिक्ता कृष्णभेदा कटम्भरा । अशोका मत्स्यशकला चक्राङ्गी शकुलादनी । मत्स्यपित्ता काण्डरुहा रोहिणी कटुरोहिणी ॥१३४॥, कट्वी तु कटुका पाके तिक्ता रूक्षा हिमा लघुः । भेदिनी दीपनी हृद्या कफपित्तज्वरापहा । प्रमेहश्वासकासास्रदाहकुष्ठकृमिप्रणुत् ॥१३५॥
