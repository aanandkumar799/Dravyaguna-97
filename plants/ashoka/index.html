Ashoka

Saraca asoca (Roxb.) Willd.
NCISM 97 · No. 4

Stem bark pieces are channelled or curved, with rough greyish-brown outer surface and reddish-brown inner surface; strongly astringent.

Verified study data

Sanskrit name
अशोक
Botanical name
Saraca asoca (Roxb.) Willd.
Family
Fabaceae
English name
Ashoka Tree
Habit
Medium-sized evergreen tree with a dense crown and drooping young foliage.
Description
Stem bark pieces are channelled or curved, with rough greyish-brown outer surface and reddish-brown inner surface; strongly astringent.
Rasa
kashaya, tikta
Guna
laghu, ruksha
Virya
sheeta
Vipaka
katu
Useful part
stem bark (Twak), seeds (Bija), flowers (Pushpa)
Indications
Asrigdara, Pradara, Yoniroga, Daha, Trishna, Apachi
Dose
Twak 20–30 g for kwatha
Anupana
Milk (as Ashoka kshirapaka).
Precautions
Strongly astringent and may cause constipation. Heavy or irregular uterine bleeding should be diagnosed before treatment.
Contraindications
Avoid in pregnancy except under specialist advice; avoid in Vibandha. Market bark is often substituted with Polyalthia longifolia, so authenticated material is essential.
Therapeutic actions
Asrigdarahara, Garbhashaya-shothahara, Sangrahi, Varnya, Vranaropana
Quick revision
A prime uterine tonic, it regulates menstruation and reduces cramps.
Shloka / classical text
अशोकः शीतलस्तिक्तो ग्राही वर्ण्यः कषायकः । दोषापचीतृषादाहकृमिशोषविषापहः ॥
Nighantu references
Bhavaprakasha Nighantu, Vatadi Varga, verses 47–48
Samhita references
